You are working on the Pope Cleaning Services Replit project.

MAIN TASK
Build and finalize a production-ready “Request a Cleaning Quote” form for the public Book Now page.

This form is the operational backbone of the business. It must reliably collect customer information, service details, property details, extras, access notes, photos and contact preferences so Pope Cleaning Services can review the request, prepare a quote and contact the customer.

The public customer must NOT see prices, GST, discounts, estimated totals or internal calculator output.

The Live Estimated Price Calculator must remain hidden from the public and reserved for future admin-only use.

Do not modify unrelated pages unless required to keep navigation working.

CURRENT PUBLIC FLOW
The public Book Now page should work as a quote request form, not a public pricing calculator.

The customer should:

1. Select the service they need.
2. Add property or facility details.
3. Select extras if needed.
4. Add schedule and access information.
5. Upload photos optionally.
6. Add contact details and preferred communication method.
7. Review the request.
8. Submit the quote request.

After submission, the business must be able to access the saved request from the admin-accessible database or backend.

Do not collect payment information.
Do not require account creation.
Do not ask for full street address too early.
Do not expose internal admin pricing fields.
