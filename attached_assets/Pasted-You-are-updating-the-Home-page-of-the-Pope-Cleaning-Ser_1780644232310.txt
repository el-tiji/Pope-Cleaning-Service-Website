* A large central number:

  * Card 4: “4”

CARD HOVER STATE

When the user hovers over a card on desktop:

* Animate the large number out.
* Reveal the step title and explanatory text.
* The transition should feel premium and smooth.

Use one of these animation approaches:

Preferred option:

* Number fades out and moves slightly upward.
* Text fades in and rises into place from below.
* Card slightly lifts upward.
* Shadow becomes slightly stronger.
* Optional thin blue border or soft blue glow appears.

Suggested CSS behaviour:

* default number:

  * opacity: 1;
  * transform: translateY(0) scale(1);
* hover number:

  * opacity: 0;
  * transform: translateY(-16px) scale(0.92);
* default content:

  * opacity: 0;
  * transform: translateY(18px);
  * pointer-events: none;
* hover content:

  * opacity: 1;
  * transform: translateY(0);
  * pointer-events: auto;
* card hover:

  * transform: translateY(-6px);
  * box-shadow stronger;
  * border-color: brand blue / light blue.

Animation duration:

* 250–350ms.
* Use ease-out or cubic-bezier for a smooth effect.
* Avoid bouncy or distracting animations.
