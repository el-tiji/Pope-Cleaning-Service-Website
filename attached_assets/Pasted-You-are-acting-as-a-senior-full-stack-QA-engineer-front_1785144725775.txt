EXECUTION MODE

Complete this work in two stages.

STAGE 1 — DIAGNOSTIC ONLY

First inspect and test the complete application without making changes.

Produce an audit report that identifies:
- issue;
- page or component;
- severity;
- how to reproduce it;
- likely cause;
- recommended correction;
- whether the correction is safe to apply automatically.

Do not begin broad corrections until the initial diagnostic is complete.

STAGE 2 — SAFE CORRECTIONS

After documenting the diagnostic:
- automatically correct low-risk and medium-risk issues;
- preserve business content and functionality;
- do not silently alter business rules;
- do not change pricing logic;
- do not delete features that may be required later;
- do not change database structures unless clearly necessary;
- do not modify authentication or security architecture without documenting the reason.

For high-risk issues:
- document them clearly;
- explain the recommended change;
- avoid destructive implementation unless required to make the public website operational.

SEVERITY LEVELS

Classify every issue using:

CRITICAL
Prevents customers from accessing the website, navigating, submitting a quote request, or causes customer data loss or security exposure.

HIGH
Major feature is broken, a primary CTA does not work, mobile experience is unusable, or important information is inaccessible.

MEDIUM
Visual inconsistency, layout problem, accessibility issue, weak validation, unclear interaction or degraded usability.

LOW
Minor spacing, typography, animation, alignment or cosmetic issue that does not block a customer.
