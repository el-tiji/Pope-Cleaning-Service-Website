AI‑GENERATED VISUAL ASSETS (TEMPORARY PLACEHOLDERS)

IMPORTANT: For now, assume that ALL images, illustrations, icons and 3D elements will be AI‑generated placeholders, NOT final photography.

GENERAL RULES
- Do NOT use random stock photography URLs.
- Instead, design the layout and components assuming that we will later drop in real AI‑generated assets that follow the same style.
- For each visual asset, do two things:
  1) Provide a meaningful, semantic file name or path (for example: `/assets/placeholders/hero-house-cleaning-3d.png`).
  2) Provide clear, descriptive alt text and comments explaining what the image should look like (scene, style, colors).
- Keep all placeholders easy to replace later (one central config or a clear assets/ folder).

STYLE OF AI‑GENERATED IMAGES AND ILLUSTRATIONS
- Use a consistent, modern 3D / isometric style across the whole site.
- Scenes should relate directly to cleaning and to Pope Cleaning Services:
  - Perth‑style houses and apartments.
  - Offices and commercial spaces.
  - Carpets, tiles, upholstery, bathrooms, glass, and outdoor areas.
  - Cleaning tools (vacuum, mop, steam machine, pressure washer, etc.).
- Visual style:
  - Clean, minimal, slightly playful but still professional.
  - Soft 3D shapes, rounded edges, subtle gradients.
  - Lighting that feels bright and “freshly cleaned”, no dark or moody scenes.

COLOR PALETTE AND COMPLEMENTARY COLORS
- Base the image palettes on the brand colors:
  - Primary blue (#2F7FD1 or similar).
  - Dark navy/blue (#004170 or similar).
  - Light bubble blue (#7EC8FF or similar).
- Add carefully chosen complementary accents to make the 3D elements pop:
  - Soft warm accent like a light peach or soft orange (#FFC999 or #FFB46A) for small details (pillows, buckets, icons).
  - Neutral whites and light grays for walls and backgrounds so the blues stand out.
- Make sure the assets never clash with the UI:
  - Avoid neon or extremely saturated colors.
  - Keep backgrounds light so text overlays remain readable.
