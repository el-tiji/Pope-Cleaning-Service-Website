# Guarantees, insurance and trust

## Satisfaction guarantee (24-hour re-clean)
If you're not happy with any area we cleaned, tell us within 24 hours of the service and we'll come back and re-clean it free of charge. No arguments — we want it right.

## 100% Bond Back Guarantee (vacate cleans)
For vacate / end-of-lease cleans: if the property manager or agent flags any cleaning item that was within our scope, we return and re-clean those areas at no extra cost to help you get your bond back.

## Fully insured
Pope Cleaning Services carries public liability insurance covering our work in your home or business.

## Police-cleared cleaners
All cleaners hold current police clearances. You can feel safe having us in your home.
