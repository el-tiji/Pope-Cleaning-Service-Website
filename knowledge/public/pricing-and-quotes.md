# Pricing and how quoting works

Pope Cleaning Services does not publish fixed prices. Every job is quoted individually, because every property and job is different.

## What affects the price
- Type of service (regular, deep, vacate, carpet, tile & grout, commercial, pressure)
- Property type and size (bedrooms, bathrooms, floor area)
- Extras requested (oven, fridge, interior windows, carpet steam cleaning, etc.)
- Condition of the property and how much build-up there is
- Access (parking, stairs, lifts)
- Frequency — regular weekly or fortnightly schedules attract discounted rates compared to one-off cleans

## How to get a quote
1. Fill in the online quote form at /quote (takes about 2 minutes) with property details, extras and preferred timing.
2. The team reviews the request — if anything is unclear they will contact you first.
3. You receive a tailored quote by your preferred contact method (phone, WhatsApp or email).
4. Once you accept, the job is scheduled. No payment is taken online.

There is no obligation — quotes are free.

## Important notes
- The assistant/chatbot cannot give prices. Only the team confirms pricing after reviewing the details.
- Quotes are valid for the details provided; if the property condition differs significantly on the day, the team will discuss any change before starting.
