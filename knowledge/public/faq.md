# Frequently asked questions

## Do I need to supply cleaning products or equipment?
No. The team brings all professional supplies and equipment, including vacuum, mop, cloths and cleaning products. If you prefer specific products used in your home (e.g. for allergies or surfaces), mention it in the quote form and we'll accommodate where possible.

## Do I have to be home during the clean?
No. Many customers provide a key, lockbox code or alarm code. All cleaners are police-cleared and keys are handled securely.

## How do I pay?
Payment is arranged directly with the team — typically bank transfer or cash after the clean. No online payment is taken through the website.

## Are you insured?
Yes — fully insured with public liability cover, and all cleaners hold current police clearances.

## What if I'm not happy with the clean?
Contact us within 24 hours and we'll re-clean the areas of concern free of charge. Vacate cleans carry a 100% Bond Back Guarantee for agent-flagged items in our scope.

## Can you clean my office after hours?
Yes — office and commercial cleans can be scheduled after hours or on weekends to avoid disrupting your business.

## How quickly can you fit me in?
It depends on the season and your suburb. Mark your urgency in the quote form ("as soon as possible") and the team will do their best — for genuinely urgent jobs, calling 0423 258 795 is fastest.

## Do you offer one-off cleans or only regular schedules?
Both. One-off cleans (spring cleans, vacate cleans, pre-event tidy-ups) are welcome, and regular weekly/fortnightly/monthly schedules attract discounted rates.
