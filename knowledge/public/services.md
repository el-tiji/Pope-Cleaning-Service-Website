# Services offered by Pope Cleaning Services

Pope Cleaning Services is a family-owned cleaning company with 7+ years of experience servicing the Perth metropolitan area. All work is quote-based and tailored to each property.

## Residential / Regular Home Cleaning
Routine cleaning to keep a home consistently fresh. Typical inclusions:
- Kitchen cleaning (benchtops, sink, stovetop, outside of appliances)
- Bathroom and toilet cleaning (shower, bath, vanity, mirrors)
- Dusting of surfaces and skirting boards
- Vacuuming carpets and floors
- Mopping hard floors
- Emptying bins and general tidy-up
Available weekly, fortnightly, monthly or as a one-off. Regular schedules attract frequency discounts.

## Spring / Deep Cleaning
A detailed top-to-bottom clean for built-up dust, neglected areas or a seasonal refresh. Includes everything in a regular clean plus:
- Skirting boards, door frames and light switches in detail
- Behind and under accessible furniture
- Interior windows and tracks (on request)
- Detailed kitchen and bathroom degrease and descale
Ideal before events, after renovations, or as a reset before starting a regular schedule.

## Vacate / End of Lease Cleaning
Move-out cleaning designed to help tenants prepare the property for final inspection. Includes:
- Full interior clean of kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas
- Inside cupboards and wardrobes
- Oven cleaning
- Interior windows, sills and tracks
- Walls spot-cleaning and skirting boards
Backed by a 100% Bond Back Guarantee: if the property manager flags any cleaning item from our scope, we return and re-clean those areas free of charge.

## Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning
Professional steam cleaning for carpets, rugs, couches and upholstery:
- Lifts stains, odours and allergens
- Suitable for homes, rentals (often required at end of lease) and offices
- Can be combined with any other service for the same visit

## Tile & Grout Cleaning
Deep cleaning for tiled areas and hard floors:
- Restores grout lines and tile surfaces
- Bathrooms, kitchens, laundries and living areas
- Removes built-up grime that regular mopping can't shift

## Office / Commercial Cleaning
Reliable cleaning for offices, workplaces and commercial spaces:
- Workstations, meeting rooms, reception and shared areas
- Kitchens/break rooms and bathrooms
- Bins, floors and touch points
- After-hours and weekend availability for minimal disruption
Suitable for offices, retail, showrooms, gyms, warehouses and other commercial facilities.

## Pressure Cleaning
Outdoor pressure cleaning for:
- Driveways and garage floors
- Patios, alfresco areas and paths
- Selected exterior surfaces
Great for restoring outdoor areas before sale, inspection or entertaining.

## Custom / Mixed Requests
Anything not listed — mixed services, special jobs, warehouses, workshops, showrooms — can be handled as a custom cleaning request through the quote form.
