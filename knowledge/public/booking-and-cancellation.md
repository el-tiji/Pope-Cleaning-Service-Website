# Booking, access and cancellations

## How booking works
1. Submit the quote form at /quote (or call/WhatsApp the team directly).
2. The team reviews your details and sends a tailored quote.
3. Accept the quote and agree a date and time window.
4. The cleaners arrive in the agreed window and complete the job.
No payment is taken online — payment is arranged directly with the team.

## Keys and access
You don't need to be home during the clean. Common arrangements:
- Leave a key in a lockbox or agreed safe place
- Provide an alarm/gate code
- Meet the cleaner on arrival for the first visit
All cleaners are police-cleared, and keys are handled securely. Include access details (parking, stairs, lift, pets) in the quote form so the team can plan properly.

## Pets
Pets are no problem — just mention them in the quote form (friendly/secured) so the cleaners know what to expect.

## Cancellations and rescheduling
Please give at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule a booking. This lets the team offer the slot to another customer. Repeated late cancellations may affect future scheduling.

## Payment methods
Payment is arranged with the team on or after completion — typically bank transfer or cash. Details are confirmed with your quote.
