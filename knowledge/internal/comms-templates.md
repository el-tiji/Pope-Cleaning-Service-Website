# Customer message templates — STAFF ONLY

Tone: warm, brief, professional Australian English. Sign off as "Kevin" or "Genny — Pope Cleaning Services". Never include internal pricing reasoning or notes. Only include a price when it has been finalised.

## Quote sent (WhatsApp)
"Hi {firstName}, it's {sender} from Pope Cleaning Services. Thanks for your quote request ({reference}). For the {service} at your {propertyType} in {suburb}, we can do it for ${amount} including {inclusions}. If that works for you, reply here and we'll lock in a time. Any questions, just ask!"

## Quote sent (email)
Subject: Your cleaning quote — Pope Cleaning Services ({reference})
"Hi {firstName},
Thanks for your enquiry. Based on the details you provided, our quote for the {service} at your property in {suburb} is ${amount}, including {inclusions}.
This is a free, no-obligation quote. If you'd like to go ahead, reply to this email or message Kevin on 0423 258 795 and we'll arrange a suitable time.
Kind regards,
{sender} — Pope Cleaning Services"

## Missing information
"Hi {firstName}, it's {sender} from Pope Cleaning Services. Thanks for your request ({reference}). To give you an accurate quote, could you let me know: {questions}? Once I have that, I'll send your quote straight over."

## Follow-up (no reply after quote, ~3 days)
"Hi {firstName}, just checking you received our quote for the {service} ({reference}). Happy to adjust anything or answer questions. If the timing's not right, no worries at all — the quote stands if you need it later."

## Job confirmation
"Hi {firstName}, confirming your {service} on {date} between {window}. {accessLine} Any changes, please give us 24 hours notice. See you then! — {sender}, Pope Cleaning Services"

## Polite decline (out of scope/area)
"Hi {firstName}, thanks so much for thinking of us. Unfortunately this one isn't something we can take on at the moment{reason}. Sorry we can't help this time, and all the best with it."
