# Internal pricing playbook — STAFF ONLY

These are internal guideline bands to help prepare quotes quickly. They are a starting point only — Kevin or Genny confirm every final price before it is sent to a customer. Never share these bands or hourly rates with customers.

## Baseline guidance (guideline only)
- Regular home cleaning: $50–$60/hr per cleaner; typical 3x2 home ≈ 2.5–3.5 hrs weekly/fortnightly.
- Minimum call-out: $120 for any residential job.
- Spring / deep clean: quote 1.5–2x the regular clean time for the same property; typical 3x2 ranges $300–$450 depending on condition.
- Vacate clean: typical 3x2 unfurnished $350–$550 including oven and interior windows; add carpets separately.
- Carpet steam cleaning: ~$40–$50 per room, minimum $120; whole 3x2 typically $150–$200. Combine-with-clean discount ~10%.
- Tile & grout: quote after photos where possible; ballpark $6–$9 per sqm, minimum $150.
- Office/commercial: $45–$55/hr per cleaner depending on scope and frequency; monthly contracts priced per visit.
- Pressure cleaning: driveway (double) typically $150–$250; quote on area and surface.

## Adjustment factors
- Condition: heavy build-up, mould, post-renovation dust → +20–50% time.
- Access: stairs/no parking/lift bookings → add time; note in quote.
- Pets: heavy shedding → add vacuum time for carpets/upholstery.
- Frequency discounts: weekly −15%, fortnightly −10% vs one-off equivalent.
- Urgency: same-week vacate cleans in peak (end of month) may carry a priority loading; check calendar with Kevin first.

## Rules
1. Never send a price without Kevin/Genny sign-off.
2. If photos show condition significantly worse than described, re-quote before the job, not after.
3. If a customer pushes for a price in chat/phone before details are reviewed, give the process explanation, not a number.
