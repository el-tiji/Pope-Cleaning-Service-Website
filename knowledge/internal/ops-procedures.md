# Operations procedures — STAFF ONLY

## Scheduling
- Regular customers keep fixed slots; avoid moving them for one-offs.
- Vacate cleans cluster at month-end — hold buffer capacity in the last week.
- Allow travel time between suburbs; group jobs geographically per day.
- After-hours commercial: confirm building access, alarms and induction requirements in writing before the first visit.

## Keys and access
- Log every key/code arrangement in the job's internal notes (lockbox code, alarm, agent pickup).
- Keys are never labelled with addresses — use the quote reference only.
- Return agent-held keys same day for vacate cleans; get a receipt where possible.

## Supplies and equipment
- Standard kit: professional vacuum, flat mop + buckets, microfibre set, bathroom/kitchen chemicals, degreaser, glass cleaner.
- Customer-preference products: note allergies or surface restrictions (stone benchtops, wood floors) in internal notes.
- Carpet machine and pressure washer are shared equipment — book them on the calendar when quoting those services.

## On the day
- Arrival window communicated the day before via preferred contact method.
- Before/after photos for vacate cleans and first-time deep cleans (helps with bond disputes and re-clean claims).
- Any damage or pre-existing issue: photo + internal note + tell Kevin same day.

## Re-clean requests (24h guarantee)
1. Apologise, don't argue; book the re-clean within 48 hours.
2. Same cleaner returns where possible.
3. Note what was missed in internal notes to prevent repeats.
