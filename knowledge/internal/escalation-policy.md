# Escalation policy — STAFF ONLY

## Escalate to Kevin (0423 258 795) immediately for
- Damage claims or anything involving insurance
- Angry customers threatening reviews/refunds/legal action
- Hoarding, biohazard, or unsafe site conditions
- Commercial contract negotiations, induction/insurance paperwork
- Any request for a discount beyond standard frequency discounts

## Escalate to Genny (0431 450 293) for
- Scheduling conflicts and staff availability
- Supplies/equipment issues
- Customer preference conflicts (products, pets, access)

## Complaint handling steps
1. Acknowledge within 2 business hours (any channel).
2. Get specifics: which rooms/areas, photos if possible.
3. If within 24h of the clean → book re-clean (guarantee applies). No charge, no debate.
4. If outside 24h → judgment call; small goodwill re-cleans are usually worth it. Check with Kevin.
5. Log everything in the quote's internal notes.

## The copilot and escalations
The staff copilot can record escalations (escalate_case tool). Recorded escalations appear in the escalations list — they do NOT notify anyone by phone. For genuinely urgent issues, call Kevin or Genny directly as well.
