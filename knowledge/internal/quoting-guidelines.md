# Quoting guidelines — STAFF ONLY

How to review an incoming quote request and turn it into a quote.

## Review checklist (in order)
1. Service + frequency selected — does it match the property described? (e.g. "vacate" for an office is probably a mistake — confirm with the customer.)
2. Property size vs rooms — if "not sure" on sqm, estimate from bedrooms/bathrooms.
3. Extras — check for time-heavy extras: oven, fridge, interior windows, carpet steam, walls.
4. Condition clues — read the free-text notes; words like "hasn't been cleaned in months", "after renovation", "mould" mean extra time.
5. Access — parking, stairs, lift, pets. No parking in CBD adds real cost.
6. Urgency — "as soon as possible" + vacate = check calendar before quoting a date.
7. Photos — if condition is unclear and the job is large, ask for photos before pricing.

## Missing information — when to contact first
Contact the customer before quoting when:
- Property size and rooms are both vague
- Vacate clean without mention of oven/windows/carpets (set scope explicitly)
- Commercial request without area or workstation count
- Custom request with unclear scope
Use the preferred contact method they selected. Keep it to 2–3 specific questions.

## Red flags
- Requests to price way below minimums — politely decline or set expectations.
- Hoarding/biohazard situations — escalate to Kevin before responding.
- Commercial contracts with special insurance/induction requirements — escalate to Kevin.

## Statuses
- new → reviewing: someone is actively looking at it.
- reviewing → quote_sent: price sent to customer (record amount in the system).
- quote_sent → accepted / declined: customer decision.
- accepted → completed: job done.
- archived: spam, duplicates, out-of-area.
